Overview

Siraat ("the App") is a free, non-commercial Islamic application built as sadaqah jariyah (ongoing charity). Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This policy explains what data is accessed, how it is used, and your rights.

In short: Siraat does not sell data, show ads, or run usage tracking. Most data stays on your device. Optional Quran.com sign-in is used only when you choose to sync Quran-related activity through Quran Foundation services. Anonymous crash reports are used to fix bugs and improve stability.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information is required to use Siraat
  • No Siraat account registration required
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No browsing or search history
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server:

  • Reading progress — your last-read position in the Quran
  • Bookmarks, collections, and notes — saved Quran verses, Quran notes, and other saved content
  • Preferences — theme, translation choice, reciter selection, dhikr counters, prayer calculation method, and notification settings
  • Cached content — downloaded Quran text, translations, audio, mushaf images, and upcoming prayer times for offline use
  • Location cache — your last known approximate location, stored locally so prayer times can continue if location services are later disabled
  • Optional Quran.com profile cache — if you sign in, basic profile details returned by Quran.com may be cached locally so your avatar and account state load quickly

Location Data

The App requests access to your device location solely to provide:

  • Accurate prayer times based on your geographic coordinates
  • Qibla compass direction to the Ka'bah

Location data is processed on-device and sent only to the AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) as latitude/longitude coordinates to calculate prayer times. No personally identifiable information is attached to these requests. Location data is never stored on any Siraat server. Siraat may store your last known location locally on your device so prayer times can continue working if GPS or location services are turned off later.

Quran.com Account Sync

Signing in with Quran.com is optional. You can continue using Siraat without signing in. When you choose to sign in, authentication is handled through the official Quran.com OAuth flow. Siraat does not receive or store your Quran.com password.

Quran.com sign-in is used to sync Quran-related features such as bookmarks, collections, notes, last-read position, reading goals, streaks, profile information, and Quran Reflect community actions where supported. This data is exchanged with Quran Foundation services only to provide the sync and community features you choose to use.

Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the following external services to provide its functionality:

  • Quran.com API (quran.com) — to fetch Quran text, translations, audio recitations, Quran Reflect content, and optional account sync.
  • AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) — to calculate prayer times, convert Hijri dates, and prefetch upcoming prayer times for offline use.
  • Hadith API (fawazahmed0) — to fetch hadith collections.
  • Sentry (sentry.io) — to receive anonymous crash and error reports for stability improvements.

These services receive only the data necessary for their function (e.g., coordinates for prayer times, chapter number for Quran text, or Quran.com account tokens when you explicitly sign in). Please refer to each service's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Crash & Error Reporting

The App uses Sentry (sentry.io) to collect anonymous crash and error reports. When the App encounters an unexpected error, the following non-personal, technical data may be sent to Sentry:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and build number
  • Error stack traces (technical details about what went wrong)
  • General device state (memory usage, battery level)

This data is fully anonymous — it contains no personal information, no location data, no user identifiers, and no content you have viewed or interacted with. It is used solely to identify and fix bugs that affect app stability. Crash data is retained by Sentry for 90 days and then automatically deleted. You can review Sentry's privacy policy at sentry.io/privacy.

Notifications

The App may send local notifications for prayer time reminders (adhan). These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external push notification service. No notification data is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Advertisements & Monetisation

The App contains no advertisements, no in-app purchases, and no subscription models. Voluntary donations to support development are accepted through external platforms (Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal) and are entirely optional.

Data Deletion

Siraat does not create its own user accounts. Most app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete local app data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App — this removes all locally stored data including bookmarks, preferences, reading progress, and cached content.
  • Clearing App Data — go to your device Settings → Apps → Siraat → Storage → Clear Data to reset the app without uninstalling.

If you used optional Quran.com sync, signing out from Siraat stops sync on that device. Your Quran.com account and Quran Reflect account-linked data are managed by Quran.com / Quran Foundation. For full deletion steps, including Quran.com profile management and Siraat support requests, visit our Data Deletion Request page.

Data Security

Since Siraat does not operate a user-data server, your local preferences, cached Quran content, cached prayer times, and last-known location remain on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings. Optional Quran.com sync data is protected and managed by Quran Foundation services.
