Overview

This page explains how Siraat users can delete app data or request deletion of account-linked data. Siraat does not create a separate Siraat account. Optional sign-in is handled through Quran.com OAuth, and the Quran.com account itself is managed by Quran.com / Quran Foundation.

Use this page for Siraat deletion requests. For Quran.com account deletion, visit https://quran.com/profile while signed in to your Quran.com account and use the Delete Account button on the profile page.

Delete Data Stored on Your Device

Most Siraat data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data at any time without contacting us:

  • Uninstall Siraat — removes locally stored app data, including preferences, cached content, bookmarks, notes, reading progress, and local prayer-time data.
  • Clear app data — on Android, open device Settings → Apps → Siraat → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Sign out — signing out in Siraat stops Quran.com sync on that device.

Request Deletion of Account-Linked Data

If you used Quran.com sign-in in Siraat, account-linked data such as your Quran.com profile, synced Quran activity, Quran Reflect posts, comments, likes, follows, saved reflections, goals, streaks, notes, bookmarks, and collections is managed through Quran.com / Quran Foundation services.

  1. Go to https://quran.com/profile and sign in with the same Quran.com account used in Siraat.
  2. Scroll to the account deletion section on the Quran.com profile page and tap Delete Account.
  3. Follow the confirmation steps shown by Quran.com. Quran.com states that deleting the account permanently removes account data, preferences, and history from the profile, and also deletes the account if the same login is used for QuranReflect.
  4. If you need help with a deletion request related to Siraat, email us at rashidiitkgp@gmail.com with the subject Siraat Data Deletion Request.

In your email, include the Quran.com username or email address used to sign in, the type of data you want deleted, and any relevant post/profile details. Do not include your password.

What Data Is Deleted or Kept

  • Deleted from your device: local preferences, cached Quran content, cached prayer times, local bookmarks, local notes, reading progress, and other local app storage after uninstalling or clearing app data.
  • Account-linked Quran.com data: deleted through the Quran.com profile page using the Delete Account button because Quran.com manages the signed-in account and Quran Reflect services.
  • Siraat support records: if you email us, we may retain the request only as long as needed to process and document the deletion request.
  • Crash diagnostics: anonymous crash/error reports are retained by Sentry for up to 90 days and then automatically deleted.
  • Required retention: limited records may be kept where required for security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or backup retention.

Response Time

We aim to respond to Siraat deletion requests within 30 days. If your request concerns Quran.com account data, we may direct you to Quran.com / Quran Foundation account tools because the Quran.com account is managed by them.
